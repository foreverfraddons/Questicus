# Credits

**Questicus Maps** (Questicus/WorldMap, and the map art copied into Questicus/Media/Textures at
release time) is built from **OpenMap** by Ji and the OpenMap contributors -
https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap - under the MIT licence (Questicus/WorldMap/LICENSE.txt,
Questicus/WorldMap/LICENSES). The map art is Blizzard Entertainment's. Thank you!

The map's Credits page in game (Questicus Maps > What's new > Credits) lists everyone who made OpenMap.
